# EloRhytm16

A 16-step drum, drone and bass sequencer for Android.

## Install on your phone
Open the **Releases** page of this repo on your phone, under "latest", and tap `EloRhytm16.apk`.
Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time.

Every push to `main` builds a new APK automatically (see the Actions tab).
New builds install over the old one and keep your saved patterns and banks.

## Editing
The whole app lives in `app/src/main/assets/index.html`. Change it, push, and a new APK is built.
