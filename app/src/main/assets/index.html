EloRhytm16

Steps: tap to switch on, drag up or down to set volume, tap a lit step to clear it. Drum faders: drag up for a harder hit, down for a softer one. Drone: lit steps hold the drone at that volume; its fader sets how far the sample-and-hold filter jumps each step, and its pitch follows the first bass note. Bass notes: drag a note box up or down to change its pitch, tap it to hear it. Banks A to D: WRITE stores the current pattern, the letter plays it. While running, the switch happens at the start of the next loop (the letter blinks until then).
